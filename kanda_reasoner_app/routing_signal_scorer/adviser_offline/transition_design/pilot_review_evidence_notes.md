# P6 - Pilot Review Evidence Design v1

This note records the design-only vocabulary for a future Pilot review evidence
packet. P6 defines field and section names that a later governed implementation
may use to present non-authoritative human review support. It does not collect,
build, validate, write, queue, persist, or record evidence.

P6 preserves the maturity chain after P5. Review evidence remains opt-in,
case-local, ephemeral, in-memory-only by design, and non-authoritative. Any
future disagreement or taxonomy language remains blocked until frozen-router
reproduction is proven under a separately governed harness implementation.

## Design-only evidence fields

1. Case-local opt-in trace.
2. Contract boundary trace.
3. Frozen-router reproduction trace.
4. Taxonomy language trace.
5. Simulation skeleton trace.
6. Critical boundary blocker trace.
7. Human review note trace.

These are field designs only. P6 does not populate them.

## Design-only evidence sections

4. Boundary and human review.

These are section designs only. P6 does not generate a packet.
